About

Built for Brawl Stars Shorts creators

What is Brawl Analytics?

Brawl Analytics is an AI analytics platform built specifically for Brawl Stars content creators publishing YouTube Shorts. It reads the CSV export from YouTube Studio and turns raw view/retention data into concrete, channel-specific insights: which formats work, which don't, and what a new video idea is likely to do before you film it.

Who is it for?

Any Brawl Stars creator posting Shorts regularly — from creators just starting out who want to find their format, to established channels who want to systematize what already works and catch weak spots before they cost views.

How does it work?

You upload your YouTube Studio "Table data" CSV export. Brawl Analytics builds a Channel Profile from your history, detects your recurring content formats with AI, and uses that profile to power the Virality Engine, Hook Analyzer, Title Optimizer, Idea Generator and Personal AI Coach — all specific to your own data, not generic advice.
