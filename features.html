Features

Everything you need to plan your next viral Short

Brawl Analytics is an AI platform built specifically for Brawl Stars YouTube creators. Analyze your channel, predict the virality of future Shorts before publishing, discover your best-performing formats, optimize titles and hooks, and receive personalized coaching powered by artificial intelligence.

🚀

Virality Engine

Answer a few questions about a video idea and get a predicted view range, a Virality Score and a confidence level, based on your channel's own historical performance and current Brawl Stars trends.

🧩

Format Detector

AI analyzes your titles and automatically discovers the recurring content formats specific to your channel, then ranks them by real performance instead of generic YouTube categories.

🎯

Hook & Concept Analyzer

Describe the first seconds of your Short and get a hook score, curiosity and scroll-stop ratings, plus three rewritten, stronger versions of your opening.

📝

Title Optimizer

Score any title for CTR potential, curiosity, clarity and SEO, and get alternative titles across categories like curiosity, competitive, funny and viral.

🧠

Personal AI Coach

A strategist that studies your entire channel history — strengths, weaknesses, publishing timing, originality risk — and gives concrete, data-grounded recommendations.

💡

Idea Generator

Get new video ideas generated from your top-performing videos and your dominant format, ready to shoot.
