Frequently asked questions

Before you upload your data

What is Brawl Analytics?

Brawl Analytics reads your YouTube Studio CSV export and transforms raw statistics into clear patterns about what makes your Shorts successful.

What data do I need to upload?

You only need the CSV export from YouTube Studio. No account connection is required. If you need a tutorial on uploading CSV, click here.

How are videos categorized?

Videos are grouped by format so you can compare similar Shorts and understand what works best.

How does the prediction work?

Before you publish, our AI compares your video idea with your Channel Profile, historical performance, detected content formats and current Brawl Stars trends to estimate its viral potential. Once you upload a draft video, Gemini AI can also analyze the video itself and provide personalized feedback before publication. Predictions and analyses are estimates, not guarantees.

Can Brawl Analytics guarantee that my video will go viral?

No. Brawl Analytics provides AI-powered predictions and recommendations based on your channel history, current trends and video analysis. These insights are designed to help you make better publishing decisions, but no AI can guarantee future views or viral success.

Is the data I upload collected?

Yes. The data you upload is securely stored and used only to provide the features of Brawl Analytics, such as channel analytics, AI-powered predictions, and personalized recommendations. Your data is never sold or shared with third parties for marketing purposes. For more information, please read our Privacy Policy.
