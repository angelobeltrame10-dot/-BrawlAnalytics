01Information we collect

We collect only what's needed to run your dashboard:

  • Account data — the email address and password you use to sign up (password never reaches our servers in plain text; it's handled by our authentication provider, Supabase).
  • Uploaded channel data — the YouTube Studio CSV export you upload, including video titles, view counts, retention percentages, watch time and publish dates.
  • Content you create in the app — custom formats, generated video ideas, and the answers you give in the Video Analysis questionnaire (originality, format, description).
  • Basic usage data — your current plan (Free or Pro) and how many AI actions (video analyses, idea generations) you've used today, needed to enforce daily limits.

02Account information

Accounts are created and managed through Supabase Authentication. We store your email address and an encrypted session token used to keep you logged in across visits. We never see or store your raw password.

03Your uploaded CSV data

titlesviewsretentionwatch timepublish date

When you upload your YouTube Studio CSV, the parsed data is saved to your account so your dashboard, formats and score persist between sessions. This data is used exclusively to power your own analytics — it is never combined with or shown to other users, and it is never sold.

Uploading a new CSV replaces your previously stored data for the affected videos; you can also start over at any time via "Upload another CSV" in your dashboard.

04AI processing

Some features — automatic format detection and personalized idea generation — send a limited amount of text (video titles and short descriptions, never raw video files) to a Cloudflare Worker we operate, which forwards the request to our AI provider, Groq.

The Worker keeps our AI provider credentials server-side; your browser never talks to Groq directly. Only the text needed for the specific analysis is sent — never your email, password, or full account data.

05Where data is stored

Your account, uploaded videos, custom formats, channel profile and generated ideas are stored in a Supabase (PostgreSQL) database, protected by row-level security policies that restrict every record to the user who owns it.

06Cookies & local storage

We don't use advertising or tracking cookies. Your browser's local storage keeps your Supabase session token so you stay logged in between visits — this is functional storage only, required for the Service to work, not for analytics or ads.

07Third-party services

  • Supabase — authentication and database hosting.
  • Cloudflare Workers — hosts the API endpoints that relay AI requests and fetch trend data.
  • Groq — processes video titles/descriptions to power AI features.

We only share the minimum amount of information required for these services to operate.

08Data security

Access to your data is protected by authenticated sessions and Supabase row-level security, meaning database queries are scoped to your user ID at the database layer, not just in the app's front-end code. Traffic between your browser and our services is encrypted in transit (HTTPS).

09Your rights

You can, at any time:

  • Delete your uploaded CSV data from your dashboard.
  • Delete individual custom formats or generated ideas.
  • Request a full export or deletion of your account data by emailing us.

10Data retention

We keep your account and channel data for as long as your account is active. Generated ideas are cached per day and naturally replaced the next time you generate new ones. If you delete your account, associated data is removed from our database.

11Children's privacy

Brawl Analytics is not directed at children under 13, in line with our Terms & Conditions. We do not knowingly collect data from users under this age.

12Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy as the Service evolves. Continued use of Brawl Analytics after an update means you accept the revised policy.

13Data deletion

You may request the permanent deletion of your account and all associated personal data at any time by contacting us at the email address below.

Once your request has been verified, we will permanently remove your account and associated data from our systems, unless we are required by law to retain certain information.
