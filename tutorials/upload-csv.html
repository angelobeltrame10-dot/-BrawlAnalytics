How to export your YouTube Studio CSV

Follow these steps to export the exact data Brawl Analytics needs, then upload it straight into your dashboard.

1

Open YouTube Studio

Go to studio.youtube.com and sign in with the channel you want to analyze.

2

Open Analytics

Click on the Analytics tab in the left-hand menu.

3

Switch to Advanced mode

Click the Advanced mode button in the top-right corner to unlock full export options.

4

Set the date range to Lifetime

In the date selector, choose Lifetime so the export covers your channel's full history.

5

Select the required metrics

Enable exactly these metrics, then click Apply:

  • Views
  • Engaged views
  • Watch time (hours)
  • Subscribers
  • Average view duration
  • Average percentage viewed
6

Download the CSV

Click the download icon in the top-right corner and choose the second option: Comma-separated values (.csv).

7

Extract the .zip file

YouTube Studio downloads a .zip folder — extract it on your device before uploading.

8

Upload it to Brawl Analytics

Inside the extracted folder, find Table data.csv and upload it in your Brawl Analytics dashboard.

Tip:

your data stays in your account and is only used to power your own dashboard — see our Privacy Policy for details.

✅ You're all set

Head back to your dashboard and drop the CSV file into the upload area to generate your first report.
